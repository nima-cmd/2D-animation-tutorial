# T3-06 — Capstone: Your First Short

**Tool:** OpenToonz (or Krita, if you decide you prefer it — see note below)
**Goal:** Combine everything so far into one small, complete, Ghibli/Frieren-flavored animated loop — your first "finished" piece, 5-15 seconds.

This is deliberately the first milestone without a rigid step-by-step, because at this point you have the components — the constraint now is *scope*, not skill.

## Scope it small on purpose

The single most common way first animation projects fail is being too ambitious. Pick something you can actually finish:

- Your T3-04 character walking across your T3-05 parallax background, in a simple looping scene (this is the "safest" option — it's mostly assembling pieces you've already built).
- A very short vignette: your character stops, does one simple secondary action (looks up, picks up your T3-02 prop, reacts to your T3-03 soot sprite), then the loop resets.
- Keep it a loop, not a story with a beginning/middle/end — loops are far more forgiving for a first finished piece and match the "Ghibli establishing shot" mood well (a quiet, atmospheric moment rather than a plotted scene).

## Suggested approach

1. **Thumbnail/plan first** — a few quick small sketches of the key moments before animating anything at full size. Fifteen minutes here saves hours later.
2. **Background pass** — bring in (or rebuild in OpenToonz) your parallax background from T3-05.
3. **Character pass** — animate your character on top, reusing/adapting your walk cycle from T3-04 rather than starting from zero.
4. **One piece of secondary motion** — now's the time to bring back something you deliberately skipped earlier (hair sway, cloth movement, the mana-orb glow/trail) — pick exactly one, not all of them.
5. **Timing pass** — watch the whole loop several times before calling it done; almost every capstone benefits from a round of "this holds a beat too long" / "this cuts too fast" adjustments.
6. **Render** — export as a video file or gif.

## On tool choice for this piece

If OpenToonz's Xsheet workflow started clicking in T1-03, use it here — that's the point of the transition. If it's still actively fighting you and slowing down the parts that aren't about the Xsheet (i.e. you're spending more energy on the tool than the animation), it's completely fine to finish this capstone in Krita instead and keep building OpenToonz comfort separately. The goal of this milestone is a finished piece, not proving the tool switch.

## Check-in

This is the milestone most worth a real review pass — bring the rendered result back, and be specific about what you're unsure of (is the walk reading naturally against the moving background? does the secondary motion look intentional or accidental? does the loop point feel seamless?). Expect a couple of revision passes; that's normal even for professionals on a first attempt at combining elements like this.
