Track 1 — Tools & workflow

The universal machinery of digital 2D animation, ending in the move to OpenToonz — the open-sourced descendant of Ghibli's own in-house software.

Track 2 — Drawing

From line control through "why does my art look flat" to drawing specifically for animation: gesture, silhouette, and the model sheet.

Track 3 — Animation fundamentals

The classic milestone sequence from the Disney tradition (Thomas & Johnston's 12 principles, Williams' Survival Kit): ball → sack → walk → scene.

Track 4 — Pen & tablet

A parallel thread, not a phase: grip and misfire fixes first, then shortcuts and two-handed flow. Grip/strain feedback belongs in every check-in.
